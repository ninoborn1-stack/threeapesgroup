01 — The idea

We don't look away. We look at the way.

Three apes: see nothing, hear nothing, say nothing. Most people know it as looking away. For us it means: we see no problem. We see only the way to the solution and the people who can walk it.

You don't have to explain how your industry works. You don't have to tell us the backstory or blame anyone. One sentence is enough: What should the end result be?

A network of developers, strategists, creatives, lawyers, artists, and labels handles the rest. Everyone sees only their part. Only one sees the whole: us.

05 — Questions

What others wanted to know beforehand.

Why are there no prices on the site?

Because every problem is different. A price on the page would be either too high or a lie. In the inquiry you choose a budget range starting at €2,500, and we tell you honestly what's possible.

How can you solve something without understanding it?

We understand enough to know who can solve it. The depth comes from the specialists in the network. You save yourself the long explanations, and we save ourselves the guessing.

Who's in the network?

150+ people from more than 15 industries. Developers, consultants, lawyers, producers, artists, labels, brands. On top of that, contacts reaching into the administrative level, through companies like cnode and the Zulano Group, which we work with. We don't name individuals. That's the point.

What is the special code?

For partners, referrals, and special cases. With the code, you can select budgets under €2,500 and partnership inquiries in the form. If you need one, you'll get it from us personally.

Can I become part of the network?

Yes. In the inquiry, choose “Join the network” and write in two sentences what you can do. We'll get in touch if it fits.
