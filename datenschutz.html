2. Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. 3. Contact by email and inquiry form

When you write to us or use the inquiry form, we process your details (name, email, optionally company and phone, your message, budget and fields) to handle your inquiry and reply to you. The legal basis is the initiation or performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.

Our mailbox and form delivery run via Febas (febas.de) on servers in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Febas. After sending, you receive a confirmation by email. To prevent spam, we store a non-traceable hash of your IP address for no more than two hours (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We delete your details as soon as your inquiry is settled and no statutory retention periods apply. If the inquiry leads to an engagement, we keep business correspondence for up to six or ten years as required by commercial and tax law.

4. Fonts, scripts and images

Fonts and scripts are embedded directly on this website. No connection to Google or other third parties is made for them. Some images are currently loaded from Unsplash (Unsplash Inc., San Francisco, USA). This transfers your IP address to Unsplash (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Browser storage

For page transitions, and so the intro animation only plays once per visit, we briefly store a value in your browser's storage (sessionStorage). It contains no personal data, never leaves your device and is technically necessary (Section 25(2) TDDDG).

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). An email to kontakt@threeapesgroup.com is enough.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
